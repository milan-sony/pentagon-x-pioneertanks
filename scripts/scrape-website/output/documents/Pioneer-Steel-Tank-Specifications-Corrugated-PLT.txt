Description Cylindrical-shaped, Modular-built, Bolted Steel Water storage tanks with
internal liner covered by a Dome Roof.
Wall Structure
ZINCALUME® G300 steel panels with corrugated wall profile.
Protective capped bolt system and fully enclosed bolt covers.
Multi-Laminated wall panel thickness per tank height.
Protective Coating
Tank panels and Roof sheets made of ZINCALUME® - coated steel
(Zinc/Aluminum Alloy), Grade AZ150.
This is a hard-wearing, thermoset coating of 150 gr/m2 deposit.
Bolting Specification Hot-Dip Galvanized, high tensile steel bolts, Grade 8.8.
M10 and M12 for the tank body and M16 for fittings.
Roof Panels ZINCALUME® G550 corrugated steel sheets, 0.50mm thick.
Roof Trusses
Hot-Dip galvanized, steel tubular sections with coating Grade AZ 200.
The roof trusses shall be bolted to tank wall and shall be self-supporting
without any ground columns.
Tank Liner
A high tear resistant, one-piece heavy duty, Reinforced PVC-Polyester
liner shall be installed inside the tank to contain the stored liquid.
The liner shall have an internal woven scrim for reinforcement, with a
tensile strength of 2600N per 5cm in the Warp direction and a coated
weight of 680gr/m2.
The Liner is suitable for liquid storage with a pH range of 4-11 and is
rated for the storage of potable water with a relevant test certificate to
the AS 2070.2 - 1992 Australian Standard.
Nozzles
Tank nozzles and fittings shall be of Hot Dip Galvanized (HDG) steel
with an outer flange connection to external piping systems. Tanks shall
be supplied with an overflow with downpipe and a scour drain.
Accessories
External Ladder with safety cage included for tanks above 4.0m off the
ground (HDG).
Lockable Roof Access Hatch with platform and handrail (HDG).
Full-Length Level Indicator with HDG column.
Roof Ventilator to be of Dynamic type (Whirlybird or similar).
Foundation Tanks shall be designed for installation onto a reinforced concrete ring
beam foundation encasing a clean compacted sand base.
Warranty Tanks to be covered by 10-Year Conditional Warranty.
