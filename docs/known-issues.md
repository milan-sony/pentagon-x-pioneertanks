### Design 1 – Editorial Warm
- All pages implemented. The marquee strip and parallax banners are CSS/transform only and pause under reduced motion; the marquee has a visible pause control.
- Product pages pass `?product=<slug>` to the contact form; the preselect runs client-side after hydration so every route stays static.
- Hero photographs are the same 1920×650 / 1920×980 originals as before (BLOCKER-3); inner-page banners reuse the old site's stock banners at 60 % opacity behind an espresso gradient.
- Preloader (2026-11-08): shown once per browser session on a cold load, for at least 0.9 s. It lowers Lighthouse Speed Index (≈ 3 s) because lab runs are always a first visit; remove it or shorten `MIN_MS` in `src/components/shared/Preloader.tsx` if raw lab scores matter more than the brand moment.
- 404 page redirects home after 3 s; the "Stay on this page" button is the WCAG 2.2.1 control for that time limit and must stay if the redirect stays.
- Home product strip auto-scrolls every 3.5 s for every visitor, including those who prefer reduced motion (client decision, 2026-11-08). It pauses on hover, keyboard focus, off screen, for 6 s after manual scrolling and via its visible pause button (WCAG 2.2.2). The rest of the site's motion still rests under reduced motion.
